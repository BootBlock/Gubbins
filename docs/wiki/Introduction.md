# Introduction

Gubbins is an app for keeping track of your **things** — and everything you might want to know
about them: how many you have, where they are, what they're worth, when they need servicing,
who you lent them to, and when to reorder.

It's built for people with *a lot of stuff to keep straight*: makers and electronics
hobbyists, workshops and studios, home organisers, collectors, and small teams who share
tools and equipment. Whether you're cataloguing a component drawer, a garage, or a props
store, the same building blocks apply.

![The Gubbins dashboard, showing inventory totals and attention widgets](images/dashboard.png)

## What makes Gubbins different

- **Local-first and fully offline.** Your inventory lives in a database inside your browser,
  on your own device. There's no account to create and no server to sign in to — Gubbins works
  with no internet connection at all, and keeps working if a service ever goes away. See
  [[How your data is stored|How-Your-Data-Is-Stored]].
- **Private by default.** Nothing is uploaded anywhere unless *you* turn on an optional
  feature and point it at *your own* storage. See [[Privacy & security|Privacy-and-Security]].
- **Installable.** Add Gubbins to your device like a native app, with its own window and an
  offline indicator. See [[Installing Gubbins|Installing-Gubbins]].
- **As simple or as deep as you need.** Track a shelf of boxes with nothing but names and
  counts, or go all the way to serialised assets with warranties, batch expiry, depreciation
  and calibration logs. Features you don't use can be hidden entirely — see [[Modular UI|Modular-UI]].

## A quick tour of the main areas

| Area | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Your landing overview — totals and anything needing attention, on a board of widgets you can rearrange. See [[Dashboard & widgets\|Dashboard-and-Widgets]]. |
| **Inventory** | The heart of the app: your [[items\|Items]], [[locations\|Locations-and-Stock]] and stock. |
| **Projects** | Group items into builds with a bill of materials and a budget. See [[Projects & BOM\|Projects-and-BOM]]. |
| **Contacts** | The people and organisations you [[lend to or borrow from\|Loans-Check-Out-and-In]]. |
| **Reports** | [[Valuation, spend, sales and stock insights\|Reports-Overview]] across your inventory. |
| **Search** | [[Find anything fast\|Search-Overview]] — full-text, a visual builder, or plain English. |

> **💡 Tip**
> Don't feel you have to set everything up at once. Add a few items, get a feel for the app,
> and switch on richer features (batches, maintenance, projects) only when you need them.
