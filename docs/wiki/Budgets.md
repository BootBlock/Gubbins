# Budgets

Give a [[project|Projects-and-BOM]] a **budget** and Gubbins tracks spending against it, so you
know where you stand and get a clear signal when a build is heading over.

**Where to find it:** on a **project** (Projects screen), when the module is enabled.

## Setting a budget

Set a target amount for a project. As you record expenses against it, Gubbins compares spend to
budget and shows the status — comfortably under, close, or **over budget**.

## Recording expenses

Log what a project actually cost — parts, materials, anything you spent — and the running total
updates. When spend crosses the budget, the project surfaces an **over-budget** status so it's
obvious at a glance rather than a nasty surprise at the end.

> **💡 Tip**
> Set the budget *before* you start buying. The point isn't to record history after the fact —
> it's to see, mid-build, when the next purchase would tip you over.

> **ℹ️ Note**
> Budgets are per **project**. For spending trends across your whole inventory over time, see the
> [[spend analytics|Valuation-and-Spend]] in Reports.
