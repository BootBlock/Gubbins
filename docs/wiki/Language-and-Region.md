# Language & region

Gubbins adapts to where you are and the language you read — controlling the **interface language**
and how **numbers, dates and currency** are formatted, all from one setting.

**Where to find it:** **Settings → Appearance → Language & region**.

## Language

The interface is available in **English** and **German** today, with more possible over time.
Gubbins picks the language from your chosen locale, and anything not yet translated falls back to
English automatically — so the app is always fully usable, whatever you pick.

You can still choose a region whose language isn't translated yet — say **French (France)** — to get
its number, date and currency formatting. The picker marks those as **formatting only**, and the
interface stays in English until a translation for that language ships.

> **ℹ️ Note**
> A region tagged *formatting only* changes how dates, times and numbers look — not the language of
> the interface. Pick a translated language (English or German today) if you want the words to change
> too.

## Region & formatting

Your locale also sets how Gubbins **formats** things:

- **Numbers** — grouping and decimal separators.
- **Dates** — day/month order and style.
- **Currency** — symbol and formatting for prices throughout the app.

Your **base currency** is guessed on first run from your device and can be changed here — it's the
currency prices are shown and totalled in across [[items|Items]], [[projects|Projects-and-BOM]] and
[[reports|Reports-Overview]].

> **💡 Tip**
> Language and formatting share **one** locale setting, so switching region gets you both the right
> words *and* the right number/date/currency style in a single change.

> **ℹ️ Note**
> Changing your base currency changes how amounts are **displayed and totalled** — it doesn't
> convert historical values at an exchange rate. Set it to the currency you actually work in.
