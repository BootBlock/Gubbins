# Branding

Make your copy of Gubbins feel like *yours*. The **Branding** settings let you dial in a custom
accent colour, add your own label beside the app name, and choose how solid or translucent the
app's surfaces look.

**Where to find it:** **Settings → Branding**.

> **ℹ️ Note**
> The name **Gubbins** itself can't be changed — Branding adds your own touches *alongside* it,
> rather than replacing it.

## Custom accent colour

The **[[Appearance & theming|Appearance-and-Theming]]** tab offers a row of ready-made accent
swatches. Branding goes further: turn **Custom accent colour** on and a **hue slider** appears,
so you can pick *any* colour for buttons, links, focus rings and the "look here" highlight.

The colour updates live as you drag, and its lightness is tuned automatically so it stays
legible in **both** light and dark mode — just like the built-in swatches. Turn it back off to
return to your chosen preset colour.

> **💡 Tip**
> The custom accent stacks with everything else — light/dark mode, Pure black (OLED) and High
> contrast all still apply on top of your chosen hue.

## Brand tagline

Add a short label — an organisation or family name, a site, a slogan — shown in a muted tone
**next to the Gubbins wordmark** in the top-left of every screen and on the home screen. For
example, a tagline of *Acme Widgets* shows as:

> **Gubbins** · Acme Widgets

Leave it blank to show just "Gubbins".

## Surface style

Sets how **opaque** the app's surfaces — item cards, dashboard widgets and panels — are:

- **Solid** — fully opaque (the default).
- **Soft** — a subtle translucency, so a hint of the background shows through.
- **Sheer** — a more pronounced translucency, letting the mode and accent tint read clearly
  through the surface.

> **⚠️ Heads-up**
> Menus, dialogs and pop-ups always stay solid so they're easy to read, and turning on **High
> contrast** (on the Appearance tab) keeps every surface solid whatever you pick here — so
> legibility always comes first.

> **ℹ️ Note**
> If you leave this on **Solid** but switch on a [[Appearance & theming|Appearance-and-Theming]]
> **background effect** (snow or rain), surfaces turn softly translucent on their own while the
> effect is running, so the drift shows faintly through — then return to solid when you turn it
> off. Choosing **Soft** or **Sheer** already shows the background through, so those are left as
> you set them.
