# Kiosk & tablet mode

Running Gubbins on a **wall-mounted tablet** in the workshop or store? **Kiosk mode** keeps the
screen awake and the interface contained, so a shared display stays usable all day without
fiddling.

**Where to find it:** **Settings → Dashboard → Kiosk & display**.

## What it does

Turn kiosk mode on and Gubbins:

- **Keeps the screen awake** with a wake-lock, so the tablet doesn't sleep mid-task.
- **Contains touch and selection** on the dashboard, so stray taps and text-selection don't
  disrupt a display people walk up to and use.

It pairs naturally with an [[installed|Installing-Gubbins]] app running full-screen in its own
window.

> **💡 Tip**
> Kiosk mode is great with a lean **[[module set|Modular-UI]]**: hide everything the shared tablet
> doesn't need, switch kiosk on, and you've got a focused, always-on inventory station.

> **ℹ️ Note**
> The screen wake-lock depends on browser/device support and only holds while Gubbins is in the
> foreground — it's a convenience for a dedicated display, not a guarantee the screen never sleeps.

## Large-format displays

On tablets and foldables, Gubbins also **widens its layout** to make better use of the extra room —
so a big screen isn't just a stretched phone view.
