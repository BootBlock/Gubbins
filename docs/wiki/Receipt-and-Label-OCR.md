# Receipt & label OCR

Photograph a **receipt** or a **product label** and Gubbins can read the text and pre-fill a new
item for you — price, date, model and serial — so you type less when adding things.

**Where to find it:** the OCR prefill option when creating an item, once it's turned on in
**Settings → Scanning & labels**.

## How it works

OCR (optical character recognition) reads the text out of a photo. Point it at a receipt or a
label and Gubbins extracts what it can — a **price**, a **purchase date**, a **model/part
number**, a **serial** — and drops them into a **reviewable** create-item draft.

- It **only fills blank fields** and **never auto-saves** — you always review and confirm before
  anything is written.
- It runs **entirely on your device** (a bundled text-recognition engine), so no photo is
  uploaded anywhere.

There are two accuracy tiers — **Fast** (smaller, quicker) and **High accuracy** (larger,
better) — selectable in Settings.

> **💡 Tip**
> OCR is best as a *head start*, not a source of truth — glance over the filled fields and fix
> anything it misread before saving. It's a time-saver, not autopilot.

> **⚠️ Heads-up**
> OCR is **opt-in** and its engine is downloaded separately the first time you use it, to keep
> the app small and fast for people who don't need it. Turn it on under **Settings → Scanning &
> labels**.

> **ℹ️ Note**
> Like everything in Gubbins, this is offline and private — the recognition happens locally and
> no receipt image leaves your device. See [[Privacy & security|Privacy-and-Security]].
