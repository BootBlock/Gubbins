# Sales & disposals

Not everything stays forever. When you **sell** something or **write it off**, Gubbins records it
properly — removing it from stock while keeping the history, and feeding the
[[sales & margin|Sales-and-Margin]] report.

**Where to find it:** an item's actions (**Sell** / **Write off**), once the **Sales & disposals**
capability is enabled ([[Modular UI|Modular-UI]]).

## Recording a sale

**Sell** an item to record it leaving for money — capture what it sold for, and Gubbins removes
the stock and logs the sale. Sold items feed the [[sales & margin|Sales-and-Margin]] report, so
your profit picture stays current.

## Writing off

**Write off** an item that's leaving *without* a sale — broken, lost, expired, given away. The
stock is removed and the disposal recorded, so your counts stay accurate and there's a clear
trail of what happened to it.

> **💡 Tip**
> Use write-off to clear [[dead stock|ABC-Turnover-and-Aging]] and [[expired batches|Batches-and-Lots]]
> honestly — it's better to record that something's gone than to leave a phantom count that
> misleads your reports.

> **ℹ️ Note**
> Selling or writing off is *permanent removal* — different from a [[loan|Loans-Check-Out-and-In]]
> (temporary, comes back) or a [[transfer|Locations-and-Stock]] (moves between your own locations).
> Every disposal is kept in the [[activity log|Activity-Log]].
