# Maintenance & servicing

Tools and equipment need looking after. Gubbins schedules **servicing** for your items, tracks
what's **due**, and keeps a log of what's been done.

**Where to find it:** the **Lifecycle** tab of an item's details, once the **Maintenance &
servicing** capability is enabled ([[Modular UI|Modular-UI]]).

## Scheduling maintenance

Set up a maintenance schedule on an item and Gubbins works out when the next service is due. A
schedule can be:

- **Time-based** — every N months (an annual PAT test, a quarterly clean).
- **Usage-based** — after so many hours of use. Gubbins accrues **checkout-hours automatically**
  as an item is [[loaned out|Loans-Check-Out-and-In]] and returned, so usage-driven servicing
  keeps itself honest without manual meter readings.

Schedules can be **per-location**, so the same kind of item serviced in different places can be
tracked separately.

## What's due, and the log

Due and overdue servicing surfaces in the [[Alerts|Alerts]] and [[Upcoming|Upcoming-Agenda]]
feeds. When you service an item, record it — building a **maintenance history** you can look back
over.

> **💡 Tip**
> Usage-based schedules pair beautifully with loans: lend a tool out, and the hours it's away
> count towards its next service automatically. Check the [[Upcoming agenda|Upcoming-Agenda]]
> before a busy period to service everything proactively.

> **ℹ️ Note**
> For **serialised** items that need structured pass/fail results (calibration, PAT, QA), use
> **[[test & calibration records|Test-and-Calibration-Records]]** alongside the free-form
> maintenance history.
