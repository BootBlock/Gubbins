# Variants & SKUs

When one product comes in several **sizes, colours or values**, you don't want a dozen
unrelated items cluttering your inventory. **Variants** link a parent item to its child
variants, so they stay grouped but each keeps its own stock.

**Where to find it:** the **Lifecycle** tab of an item's details, once the **Variants & SKUs**
capability is enabled ([[Modular UI|Modular-UI]]).

## What variants are for

A **variant** is *the same product in a different flavour*:

- Resistors: 1 kΩ, 10 kΩ, 100 kΩ.
- A T-shirt: S / M / L, in black or white.
- Filament: the same brand in a dozen colours.

You keep one **parent** item for the product and add **child** variants under it — each with its
own stock, SKU/part number, price and details. Browsing stays tidy, but you can still see the
whole family together.

> **ℹ️ Note**
> The stock lives on the **variants**, not the parent — so a parent never shows as low or out of
> stock, never joins the [[shopping list|Reorder-and-Shopping-List]], and doesn't count towards
> your inventory's total value on its own account. Set
> [[low-stock thresholds|Low-Stock-and-Gauges]] on the variants you actually reorder. (Its other
> [[alerts|Alerts]] — an expiry date, an overdue loan, servicing due — still apply as normal.)

> **ℹ️ Note**
> Variants are for the **same product**. That's different from:
> - **[[Kits & bundles|Kits-and-Bundles]]** — an item *made of* several other items.
> - **[[Related items|Tags-Attachments-and-Related-Items]]** — separate items that *work with*
>   each other.
> - **[[Substitutions|Tags-Attachments-and-Related-Items]]** — items that can *stand in* for one
>   another.

> **💡 Tip**
> Use a variant's SKU/part number to hold the specific ordering code for that size or colour —
> handy when you [[reorder|Reorder-and-Shopping-List]] or [[scan|Camera-Scanning]] a specific
> variant.
