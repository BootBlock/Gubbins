# Continuous scan & batch actions

When you've got a *pile* of things to process — a delivery to receive, a shelf to relocate, a kit
to sign out — scanning them one at a time is slow. **Continuous scan** keeps the camera running
and collects everything you scan, so you can act on the whole batch at once.

**Where to find it:** the continuous mode inside the [[scanner|Camera-Scanning]] (**Scan**).

## Scanning a batch

In continuous mode, Gubbins keeps decoding without stopping after each hit — scan one code after
another and each recognised item is added to a running list. You see what you've captured as you
go.

## Acting on the whole batch

Once you've scanned a batch, apply one action to **all** of them together, for example:

- **Move all** to a [[location|Locations-and-Stock]] — great for putting a delivery away.
- **Check out all** to a [[contact|Contacts]] — sign out a whole kit in one go.

> **💡 Tip**
> Continuous scan turns a tedious job into a quick sweep: scan every box off the van, then
> **move all** to *Receiving* in one action instead of editing each item.

> **ℹ️ Note**
> A code you scan twice isn't counted twice — the batch tracks distinct items, so a quick
> double-beep won't throw your numbers off.
