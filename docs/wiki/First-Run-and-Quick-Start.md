# Quick start

This page takes you from a blank slate to your first tracked item. It should take just a
couple of minutes.

> **ℹ️ Note**
> If you haven't opened Gubbins yet, see [[Installing Gubbins|Installing-Gubbins]] first.

## 1. The first-run setup

The very first time you open Gubbins, it offers a short, **skippable** setup:

- **Choose the modules you want.** Gubbins can hide whole areas it thinks you may not need
  (Projects, Purchase orders, Bookings, and more) so the app starts lean. Pick a preset, or
  just **Skip** to keep the standard set on — you can change any of this later on the
  [[Modular UI|Modular-UI]] screen. (A couple of modules, such as
  [[Users|Users-and-Accounts]], start switched off and are never turned on by a preset.)
- **Your currency and region are guessed** from your device, so prices and dates look right
  straight away. You can adjust them under [[Language & region|Language-and-Region]].

> **💡 Tip**
> Not sure what you'll use? Skip the chooser. Nothing is switched off, and you lose nothing by
> turning modules off later — hidden features keep all their data.

## 2. Add your first item

Open **Inventory** from the menu and select **Add item**. Give it a name — that's the only
required field — and select **Create item**.

![The Add item dialog, with a name and a tracking mode](images/add-item-dialog.png)

A few fields are worth knowing about even now:

- **Tracking** decides how Gubbins counts this item. Leave it as the default for a simple
  count; the full list is on [[Tracking modes|Tracking-Modes]].
- **Initial quantity** is how many you have right now.
- Everything else — photo, category, price, location, notes — is optional and can be added
  later by editing the item.

> **💡 Tip**
> You don't have to create items one at a time. If you already have a list in a spreadsheet or
> another app, you can paste or import it in one go — see [[Export & import|Export-and-Import]]
> and [[Migrating from another tool|Migrating-from-Another-Tool]].

## 3. Add a location (optional but recommended)

Locations are *where things live* — a room, a shelf, a drawer, a bin. In Inventory, select
**Add location**, name it, and (optionally) give it a colour and a parent location to build a
hierarchy like *Garage → Shelf A*.

Once you have locations, you can assign items to them and move stock between them. The full
picture is on [[Locations & stock|Locations-and-Stock]].

## 4. Find your way around

- The **menu** (top of every screen) jumps to any area.
- The **dashboard** shows totals and anything needing attention.
- **Search** (and the command palette) finds items instantly — see
  [[Search overview|Search-Overview]].
